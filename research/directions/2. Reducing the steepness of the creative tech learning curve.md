# First thoughts & reflexions

A remarque i've made to myself while learning creative tech is how hard it is to learn theses skills and how much ground knowledge it require. You need to be good in general IT. Manage all sort of data and consider theses as raw data source, which is a hard to gain mental model. By data I mean images, video, audio, text, bin, spacial data (3D models, coordinates, matrices). To know what is all of theses data source, manage and create them, make art with it. The expensive knowledge in design, code, numeric creation is quit hard to gain, and it create a strong barrier to entry.

# What & For What ?

The goal would be to allow a newcomer to have fun and play with tech without much need of understanding. In other terms creating a think enough layer of abstraction that allow a begginer to have fun. But permeable enough so that an experience tech artist can also quilky play and also have fun and create prod ready art.

# For Who ?

The main target is newcomer to tech art, that, have a basic understanding and some desired productions. Mainly due to consumption of social networks. They can be reach through social networks like discord communities.
# Where & When ?

It's something than could take a place in the current ecosystem, where there is only some small accesible tools but they are focus on one things (eg. dithering). As a first though I can easly think about a touch desinger plugin or a new tool that could be run in the web or a local soft.
