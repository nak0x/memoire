## Overview

This folder gathers the emerging research directions that structure the memoire, each note sketching an initial problematic and the reasoning behind it. The first direction interrogates the place of digital devices in alternative approaches to childhood education, questioning why such movements broadly reject screens rather than challenging the corporate logics that shape how those screens are used. The argument holds that the harm lies not in the technology itself but in platforms engineered for attention capture and passive consumption rather than genuine exchange, creation, or critical thought.
