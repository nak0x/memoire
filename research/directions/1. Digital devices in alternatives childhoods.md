# Firsts thoughts & reflexions
The first reflexion behind this direction is that in my current knowledge, none of the alternatives ways of making kids grows is really happy that kids spends times on screens. Most of them pushes kids to go out and do things in what they call the "real world" But doing so, is for me, weird and reducing.

The first arguments that I often ear is that screen isolate child and make them stupid. And I have to say that this is true, in the current context, at least I think so too.
But it's not the due to screens nor it does to the child or the human developing brain. I think that is more due to what the child do on theirs screens and what platforms and big corporations are creating.

In my current vision of our digital world, big corps, are into creating new spaces for colonizing our time with advertising and self validating content more than creating real spaces for discussions, pair work and inspiration, mental and spiritual evolution. And that's understandable. In a world of consumerism and capitalism it does not make sens to waste time creating a non-profit space that people could use to critic the people that have create the said space. Nor it make sense to allow real un-moderated content creation because it's putting responsibility on the users to be critic with what he see and to think about the content he create. And that's relying on the user to be "conscious". With is dangerous for the capitalism as it could lead the users to be scared and more aware of the manipulation and informations deformations that are maid to make theirs life "easier". (With is another therm to say, "a way to control the mass with it discovering it").

But here we touch what's could be the first key of this study, having people that are really capable or living and navigating in an un-moderated space. Which implies a tons of things, but to do so, a person need to be critic with everything, thinks deeply about subjects, have a good general culture, accepts non validating arguments and be able to accept criticism. And to do so, in my opinion a person need to have a lots of what we call "soft skills".

The seconds arguments is that spending more and more times on screen make people and more specifically child unable to experience the outdoor. And able to be a maker, an artisan. To be resourceful. But this is easily answer at least I currently think so, and it stand in one question, "who as never follow a DIY tutorial ?". And this, is very strong, as it demonstrate knowledge sharing and accessibility. The downside of that is, that strictly following without being creative or conscious, do not create skills. It only create automatism without creating flexibility. The person will be very fast to be able to do something, but unable to create another in the same veine but with the same quality/level of making.

## Inspiration & starting point
[Summerhill](https://www.summerhillschool.co.uk/) - A better way to educate

When I was in middle school, i've red a book about the Summerhill school, and I think that this lecture is the things that have start it all. It was the first time for me that I was introduce to an alternative ways of educating and allowing children to grow. As ate person that has been hurt and that profoundly hate the traditional educational system, seeing an alternative have plant a seed for future reflexions and divagations. In my currents works and day to day life theses reflections still lives in me and pushes me to thinks about how can I council my hobbies and passions about digital worlds and arts, with my desire for alternative and more connected and naturals/wilds ways of growing and living. What is the places of a computers in a wilds and "disconnected life" ? Why did we need to live disconnected to live well and stress-less ? Those are questions that I can't find answer, but I think a child growing in an environment that does not make a difference between a "digital" world and a "real" world, as what exists in the digital world exists as much as wood sculpture and can give as much to a kid. I do thinks that those kids could have an answer.
