# First thoughts & reflexions

It's been a while since I want to work on procedural generation of explorable environments. With the raise of world models, AI is also starting to take over world generation. It's only the begging but world models are understanding environments and able to create them. This allow them to have a deep understanding in the concepts and it's evolution through the actions. This create new perspectives but i'm also deadly scare of the AI slope and the lack of fun and passion it creates.

The goal would be to create natural looking structure that attempt to demonstrate the complexity of nature that, look random but is not. Creating explorable spaces from raw data. Some sorte of frontiers breaking system that digest analog data to produce digital form of art.

## What & For What ?

I think it would take the shape of some lib or some touch designer plugin. The goal of this is to allow the creation of such mathematical generation sourced from the analog complexity. Giving form to the slight variations and harmonies of lifes. Making it easier to emphase thoses small details take make the analogue so textured and interesting. Create a tool for my artistic practices, that could also benefits others vjs or tech artists create visuals from theirs own analogue sources.

## For who ?

That project is more of a niche tool for vjs and tech artist that dont like the stereotypical techno look of vjing with laser beam, strong colors and overstimulating visuals that are creating brain noise. It aims to help creator to create and experiencer to experience a more subtle natural looking vjing. Experience that can take a place in other music styles.

## Where & When ?

As an imaterial production this does not have a physical where but, it's aim to be place as a tool in vjing workflows. So either as a dedicated softwear, a piece of plugin, some sort of website idk. It can totaly take place in the current ecosystem. Discussing a bit with vjing and tech artist communities reveal an interest in such a tool.
